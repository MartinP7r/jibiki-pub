Privacy Policy

Katachi (Kanji Study App) · Last updated November 27, 2026

Overview

Katachi is an indie Japanese kanji study app developed by Martin Pfundmair. Your privacy comes first: Katachi does not require an account, does not display ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share your personal data with data brokers.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your dictionary lookups, handwriting practice progress, spaced-repetition (SRS) study history, streaks, bookmarks, custom study lists, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device (and within your device's local App Group container so Home Screen widgets can display your daily kanji and study progress). We do not operate a server that stores your study records.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Katachi offers optional paid upgrades ("Kanji Pro") processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to validate App Store receipts and manage entitlement status across devices.

  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user identifier and App Store transaction receipts.
  • We do not receive or store your payment card details or Apple Account billing information.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in Apple Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Notifications & System Integrations

If you opt into daily study reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's UserNotifications framework. Spotlight search indexing also runs entirely on-device.

Children's Privacy

Katachi does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
